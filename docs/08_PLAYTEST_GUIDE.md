   Ensure you are testing from the `playtest-readiness` branch.

## 2. SUPPORTED CONTROLS
- **Desktop (Keyboard & Mouse):**
  - `WASD` / `Arrow Keys`: Maneuver vessel.
  - `Left Click (Hold)`: Chrono-Stasis time dilation & trajectory aim.
  - `Left Click (Release)`: Execute Hyper-Slash kinetic dash (20 Energy).
  - `Right Click` / `Spacebar`: EMP Repulse shockwave (35 Energy).
  - `Key Q`: Chrono Parry timing window (180ms active, 15 cost with 5 bonus refund).
  - `Key F`: Overdrive Mode (when 100% charged).
  - `Esc`: Pause / Settings menu.
- **Mobile (Touch Devices):**
  - Left Screen Zone: Dynamic virtual joystick for 360-degree vector flight.
  - Right Screen Zone: Responsive `FIRE`, `HYPER-SLASH / PARRY`, and `EMP` buttons.

## 3. PLAYTEST OBJECTIVES (WAVES 1 TO 10)
- **Wave 1–2:** Verify basic movement, plasma pulse, and the energy bar. Did you reach Level 2 and obtain an augment upgrade by early Wave 2?
- **Wave 3–5:** Test projectile parry timing against Orbiters. Does parrying feel skillful rather than accidental?
- **Wave 6–9:** Evade Dasher charge lines and track Phantom teleportation.
- **Wave 10:** Defeat the Apex Overlord boss. Test EMP interaction (verify boss cannot be indefinitely stun-locked).

## 4. FEEDBACK QUESTIONS
1. Did you run out of Chrono Energy, and was it clear why?
2. Did the parry timing window feel responsive or too tight/forgiving?
3. Which augments felt most impactful on your playstyle?
4. Did any waves feel unfair or visually obscured?
5. How would you rate the overall difficulty on a scale from 1 (too easy) to 10 (punishing)?
